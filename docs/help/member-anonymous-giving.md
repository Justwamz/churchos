# Giving anonymously

When you give a tithe, offering or project gift in the app, you can switch on **Give anonymously**. You can do the same for a regular gift.

## What your church sees
Your church does not see your name or your number. For anonymous gifts it sees only a total for each day, for example "Anonymous, 3 gifts". It cannot tell which gift was yours.

## What stays with you
- You can see your own anonymous gifts in your giving history, marked **Anonymous**, and you get your own receipt.
- An anonymous gift does not count toward a pledge, and it is not in the giving record your church keeps. It cannot be turned into a named gift later.
- If you paid the wrong church, you can still ask for a reversal.
- ChurchOS, the company that runs the app, can look up who made a gift only for a refund, a fraud check or a legal request. It needs a written reason, and the look is recorded. Your mobile money provider also knows your number, as with any payment.

# My giving record

Your church keeps a record of the gifts it can link to you: your named gifts, regular gifts, and any cash you gave that the church recorded for you. Under **Give**, **My giving record** shows it with totals for each year.

## It cannot be changed
Lines are never edited or deleted. If a gift is reversed, a new line cancels it out.

## Who can look
Only a few people at your church, with a special right (usually the treasurer and the owner), can open your record. Every time someone does, or downloads it, ChurchOS records who, when, and why. You can see that list under **Who has looked at my giving record**.
