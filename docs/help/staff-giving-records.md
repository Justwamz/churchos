# Members' giving records

## Who can open one
Only people with the right **See members' giving records**. The owner and the treasurer have it. The branch admin, pastor and secretary do not, and nobody can give a right they do not hold. You can grant it under **Team**.

## Open a record
On a member's profile, choose **Giving record** and say why: a statement was requested, a pastoral visit, an audit or review, reconciling the books, or another reason with a note. You see every gift linked to the member, cash the church recorded for them, and reversals, with totals by year and purpose. **Download CSV** is also recorded.

Every look and download is written to the audit log and to a list the member can read. The record cannot be changed or deleted. A reversed gift is cancelled by a new line. The green **Record intact** badge shows the chain of lines checks out, and **Check the whole ledger** tests the church's whole record.

## Anonymous gifts
Members can give anonymously in the app. You see these only as one total for each day, branch and purpose (in Collections and settlements), never who gave or when. They are not in any member's record and cannot be revealed.

## Cash from people you know
When you count cash, you can name the members who gave, with an amount each (together no more than the count). The amounts go into their records only after a second person signs off the count. Cash from people you do not name stays an ordinary total.
