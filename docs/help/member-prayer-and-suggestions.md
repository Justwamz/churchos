# Prayer requests

Choose **Prayer** and write your request. You decide who sees it:
- **The prayer team only.** Only you and the people on the church's prayer team can read it.
- **A group I belong to**, **my branch**, or **the whole church.**

You can send it without your name. It is then shown as **A member**, to everyone, including the church leaders. People tap **I prayed**, and you see how many have. When your prayer is answered, choose **Mark as answered** and say thank you. You can take a request down at any time. Requests show for 60 days (14 days after you mark them answered), and the words are removed after a year.

The prayer team can take a request off the shared lists if it should not be there. You are told, and you still see it.

# Talking to a pastor or a counsellor

There is no chat. You book a time with a pastor or counsellor from **Bookings**, like any other booking. Once they say yes, they can add a **meeting link** (Google Meet, Zoom or similar) for a call or video meeting. You see **Join the meeting** on your booking. Only you and the people who look after the booking can see the link. Nothing is recorded.

Before you book, read the notice about what is private. What you say to a pastor or counsellor stays between you. Church administrators and ChurchOS staff can see that a booking exists, not what was said. If someone may be at serious risk of harm, or a child may be being hurt, the church has to act to keep them safe. If you are in danger now, call the emergency number shown on the screen. The helplines are listed there too.

# The suggestion box

Choose **Suggestion box**, pick a category, and tell your church what you think. You can send it **without your name**: the church sees the suggestion but not who sent it, and you can still see its answer in **My suggestions**. The church replies with a status (under review, done or not possible). Some answers are shared with everyone in the branch under **You said, we did**.
