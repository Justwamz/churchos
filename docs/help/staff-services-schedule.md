# The weekly schedule

You need the right to **Manage services and attendance**.

## Create a schedule
1. Open **Services** and choose **Set up a schedule**.
2. Give it a name (Sunday Service, 10:30), choose the branch, the day or days, the start time and how long it lasts.
3. Paste your **YouTube live link** if you stream. ChurchOS then gives every service a link that members and visitors can share, and counts who watches.
4. Choose how far ahead to create the services: this week, this month, the rest of the year, or until a date.

Check-in opens 20 minutes before the start and closes 2 hours after it. You can change both when you set it up.

## Change it later
- **Change** a schedule to rename it, move the time, or change the stream link. Tick *Apply to all coming services* to change every coming service at once.
- **Extend** it when you want more weeks.
- **Stop** it. Coming services that nobody has checked in to are cancelled.
- On a single service you can edit, move, cancel or bring it back. **Add a special service** for Easter, a funeral service or a one-off.

## The reading and notices of the day
Open a service and choose **Reading and notices**. Anyone with the right can add the reading (with its reference), a theme, and the notices. Choose when members see it: straight away, a day before, an hour before, or 20 minutes before the service. Members also see it right after they check in.

## The welcome message
Under **Welcome message**, write the SMS that every first-time visitor gets automatically. You can use {name}, {church}, {branch} and {service}. Switch it off at any time. It is paid from the branch's SMS balance, and a message over 160 characters costs more than one SMS.
