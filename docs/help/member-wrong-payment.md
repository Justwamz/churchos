# I paid the wrong church or the wrong amount

## How to ask for a reversal
1. Open **Help** in the member app, then **Payment help**.
2. Copy the confirmation message you got from M-Pesa or Airtel Money and paste it in the box. It must include the transaction code.
3. Tap **Send**.

ChurchOS finds the payment from the transaction code and checks it against its own records. We never act on the pasted text alone, because messages can be forged. You can only ask about payments made from your own phone number.

## What happens next
A ChurchOS agent reviews the request. Large amounts need two agents to approve. If it is approved, the money goes back to the phone that paid.

## What do I get back?
- The church's fee is returned in full.
- Charges taken by the mobile network, such as M-Pesa or Airtel Money, are **not** refunded by ChurchOS, because ChurchOS does not receive them.

## How long do I have?
Ask within 72 hours of the payment. Payments held in the holding account because of a wrong church code can be reversed at any time until they are refunded automatically after 48 hours.
