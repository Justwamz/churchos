# Regular giving

Members set up regular gifts in the app (**Give > Give regularly**): weekly, every two weeks or monthly. On the day, a prompt goes to their phone and they enter their PIN, so a gift happens only when they complete it. A missed prompt is sent again three times over two days, then marked missed.

You need the permission to see collections. Open **Regular giving** to see:
- **Active plans**, the **expected value each month**, what was **collected this month**, and how many gifts were **missed**.
- A list of plans with the member, branch, what the gift is for, the amount, how often, the next prompt, and how many gifts were completed and missed. Filter by status or branch, or search by name or member number. Download it as a CSV.

You cannot change a member's plan: they look after it themselves. Phone numbers are not shown. Gifts from plans appear in collections like any other gift, with the usual receipt.
