# Giving regularly

Set up your tithe or offering once, and we remind you on the day. You are **never charged without doing something**: a prompt arrives on your phone and you enter your PIN, like a normal gift.

## Set one up
1. Open **Give** and choose **Give regularly**, then **Set up a regular gift**.
2. Choose what it is for (tithe, offering or a project), the amount, and how often: weekly, every two weeks or monthly. For monthly, choose a day from 1 to 28, or the last day of the month.
3. Choose when to start, the time (10:00 am unless you change it), and M-Pesa, Airtel Money or T-Kash. Cards cannot be used yet, because a card cannot be charged without you.
4. Tick **Give the first gift now** if you want to give straight away. The regular prompts then start after today.

## On the day
A prompt arrives on your phone at the time you chose. Enter your PIN. Your gift and receipt appear as usual. If a gift is to a project you pledged to, it counts toward your pledge.

If you do not complete it, we send it again after 3 hours, then the next day and the day after. After that the gift is marked missed, you are told by SMS and in the app, and your plan carries on with the next date.

## Change, skip, pause or stop
Open the plan to change the amount, time, number or end date (a new time starts from the next prompt), **Skip the next gift**, **Pause** (nothing is sent until you resume, and you do not owe the gifts you missed), or **Stop** for good.

You can have up to five regular gifts.
