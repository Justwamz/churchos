# Confirming your email

## Who must do it
If your role receives alerts, for example anyone who can see wallets, you must have a confirmed email address before you can use the app. Low balance alerts and other important notices go to that address, so it has to be real and yours. Staff who do not receive email alerts do not need an email at all.

## How it works
1. After you sign in you will see a window asking for your email. It cannot be closed until you finish.
2. Enter your email and choose **Send code**.
3. Open the email and type the 6-digit code. The code works for 30 minutes and you have five tries.
4. If it did not arrive, choose **Send again**. You can ask once a minute.

In this local test build no real email leaves the system. The code is shown in the window under **Development only**, and the message is in the Email outbox in the admin app.

## Changing your email
Open **My profile** and enter the new address. It stays unconfirmed until you enter the new code, and alerts go only to confirmed addresses. You cannot remove your email while your role needs one.
