# Buying tickets and showing them at the door

## Buy tickets
Some events sell tickets. On **Home**, under **Coming up** or **Events**, you will see **Get tickets**, or you may find it through an advert.

1. Choose how many of each ticket type you want.
2. You see the price, a small service fee and the total before you pay.
3. Pay by M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash or card. Free tickets need no payment.

Your tickets are held for 15 minutes while you pay. If you do not pay in time, they are released.

## Your tickets
Your tickets arrive in **My tickets** straight away, and you get a message. Each ticket has its own **QR code**. If you bought for friends, you can open each ticket and show or send it. The tickets are in your name.

## At the door
Open the ticket and show the QR code. Staff scan it. A ticket works once, so only share it with the person who will use it.

## If the event is postponed
Your tickets stay valid for the new date, and you are told by email, SMS and in the app. If you cannot make the new date, you can ask for a refund from your ticket page or **My tickets**.

## If you cannot go
If the church allows refunds for this event, open **My tickets** (or the link in your email) and choose **Cancel and ask for a refund**. The church looks at each request and tells you. Your tickets still work until it decides. If it approves, the money goes back to the number or card you paid with, less any charge the payment provider keeps, and you are told when it has been sent. Free tickets are cancelled straight away. If the event is cancelled, you are told and the church arranges your refund.
