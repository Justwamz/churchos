# Messaging wallets, top-ups and low-balance alerts

Messages to members by SMS and WhatsApp are paid for from prepaid wallets. In-app messages are free.

## One wallet for each channel
Your church has an **SMS** wallet and a **WhatsApp** wallet. An SMS costs KES 1.00 per 160-character part. A WhatsApp message costs KES 3.00. The prices are set by ChurchOS.

## Who pays: shared or per branch
On the **Integrations** page your church chooses one of two ways:

- **Shared:** the whole church uses one SMS wallet and one WhatsApp wallet.
- **Per branch:** every branch is its own cost centre with its own SMS and WhatsApp wallets. When a message goes to people in a branch, that branch's wallet pays. A branch does not depend on headquarters.

Headquarters can **move credit** to a branch wallet, and back. Switching modes never removes a wallet or its balance.

## Seeing balances
Anyone whose role can **view wallets** sees every wallet they are responsible for on the **Messaging wallets** page, with the balance, about how many messages it buys, and a status of Healthy, Running low or Empty.

## Topping up
1. Choose **Top up** on a wallet and pick an amount, from KES 100 to KES 50,000.
2. Choose **M-Pesa** (a prompt appears on the phone) or **Card** (you pay on the payment provider's secure page, so card details never reach ChurchOS).
3. The balance updates as soon as the payment arrives. There is no fee on top-ups.

You can also top up by paybill, using the reference shown on the wallet, for example `SMS#GRC001`.

## Low-balance alerts
Each wallet has a warning level. When the balance falls below it, and again when it reaches zero, everyone who can view the wallet gets an alert in the app and by email. Add your email in **My profile** to receive them. You get one alert for each drop, not one for every message. A top-up above the warning level resets it.

## What happens when a wallet is empty
Nothing breaks. Receipts and messages still go out in the app. SMS or WhatsApp to people paid for by that wallet is skipped until it is topped up, and the sender is told which wallets were short.
