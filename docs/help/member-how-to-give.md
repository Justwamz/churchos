# How to give to your church

You can give your tithe, an offering or a gift to a project from your phone in under a minute.

## Give from the giving page or the app
1. Open your church's giving page, or the **Give** tab in the member app.
2. Choose what you are giving for: tithe, offering, or a project.
3. Enter the amount (from KES 10 up to KES 150,000 per gift) and your phone number.
4. Choose how you pay: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash or card.
5. Tap **Give**. A prompt appears on your phone. Enter your PIN to finish.

When the payment is complete you see a receipt with a receipt code. Keep it if you ever need to ask about the payment.

## Does the church pay a fee?
Some churches take the processing fee from your gift, and some ask you to pay it on top. If you are asked to pay it on top, the screen shows the fee and the total before you confirm. The church always receives the amount you chose.

## If the prompt does not appear
Check that the phone number is correct and has network. You can try again. If money was taken but you see no receipt, open **Help** in the member app and use **Payment help**.
