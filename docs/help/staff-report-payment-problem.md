# Reporting a payment problem

Use **Help with a payment** to ask ChurchOS to reverse a payment, for example a duplicate gift or a gift sent to the wrong church.

## Two ways
- On the **Collections** page, choose **Report a problem** on a row and give a reason.
- On the **Help with a payment** page, paste the confirmation message that contains the transaction code.

## What happens
A ChurchOS reviewer checks the payment against its records. Above KES 50,000 two reviewers must approve. If approved, the sender is refunded, the platform fee is returned, and network charges are not refunded.

If the payment was already paid out to your church, the amount is deducted from a future payout.
