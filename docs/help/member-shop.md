# Buying from the church shop

Your church's shop sells things such as Bibles, rosaries and books. You can order in the app and collect them at the branch.

## Order
1. On **Home**, tap **Shop**. Choose a category if you like.
2. Tap an item to see it, choose how many, and tap **Add to basket**.
3. Open your basket and tap **Checkout**.
4. Choose how to pay: **M-Pesa**, **Airtel Money**, **T-Kash** or **Card**. For mobile money use the number of that network. You will get a prompt on your phone.

Your items are held for 30 minutes while you pay. If the payment does not arrive, they go back on the shelf and you can order again. You can have up to three unpaid orders, and up to 10 of one item.

## Collect
When your payment arrives you get a **collection code**. Collect your order at the branch shop and show the code. You will get a message when your order is ready. You can see your orders and their codes under **My orders**.

## Adverts that open the shop
Some adverts on Home take you straight to an item, or to a checkout with the item already in your basket. You can still change the quantity or close it. If the item is sold out you will be told, and you are not charged.

## If something goes wrong
If you paid and the order does not show as paid, wait a minute and refresh. For any problem, speak to the shop or use **Help**. Refunds are arranged by the church.
