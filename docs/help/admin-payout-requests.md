# Approving payout account changes

The organisation owner requests the change, and you are the second approver.

## Before approving
Confirm the request through a known contact for the church, not through the details in the request. Account-swap fraud is the main risk here.

## After approval
A 72 hour cooling-off period starts. Payouts keep going to the old account until it ends, then the new account takes over automatically. Rejecting a request leaves the old account in place.
