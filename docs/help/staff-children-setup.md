# Setting up children's classes

You need **Manage children's classes**.

## Classes and teachers
1. Open **Children** > **Classes** and choose **Add a class**. Give it a name (Sunday School, Nursery), a room and the branch.
2. Choose the teachers. Anyone with **Check children in and out** for that branch can be added: give it under **Team**, or give them the "Children's Teacher" role. A class can have more than one teacher, and they can all scan at the same time.
3. A teacher sees only the classes they teach. You can switch a class off when it has no open session.

## Branch location
On **Classes & rooms**, choose **Use my current location** while standing at the church, and keep the distance at 300 m unless the grounds are large. From then on a child can only be checked in when the teacher's device is within that distance. A branch with no location is not checked. You can remove the location at any time.

## Guardians and approved people
Children are linked to their guardians in the member records. A guardian lists the people who may collect their child in the member app. Nobody else can collect.

## Late pick-up
When you open a session you can change two times (they start at 15 and 30 minutes after the finish time): when the guardians are told, and when the church office is alerted.

## The log
**Children** > **Log** records every scan, check-in, hand-over, refusal, SMS, alert and escalation with the time, the teacher and their device, where the teacher was (and the guardian's phone when its location was used), the guardian and their device, and the children. Search it, filter by date or kind, and download it as a CSV. The same events are in the audit log.

## Reports
**Children** > **Reports** lists each session with how many children came, how many were collected late and how many alerts went to the office.
