# Collections reports and the platform fee

## Filtering and exporting
Use the date, branch, purpose and method filters to narrow the list. **Download CSV** exports exactly what you see.

## Gross, fee and net
- **Gross** is the amount the member paid.
- **Fee** is one combined platform fee for the transaction.
- **Net** is what your church receives.

Depending on your church's setting, either you absorb the fee (net is lower than the gift) or the member pays it on top (net equals the gift). Ask your organisation owner which applies.

## Cash rows
Cash that was counted and signed off appears as method **cash**, with status **recorded**. The platform fee for cash is charged against your church's balance after sign-off, because the cash itself never passes through ChurchOS.
