# Moderating adverts

Churches publish adverts to their own members, after approval by a second person at the church. As the platform you can see every advert and remove any of them.

## Taking one down
Choose **Take down** and give a reason. The advert disappears from the member app immediately and the church is told why. A taken-down advert cannot be switched on again, the church creates a new one. The action is recorded in the audit log with your name.

## What to look for
- Artwork that imitates a bank, mobile money, a government body or another church.
- Offers of loans, investments or anything that asks members to send money.
- Paid adverts where the advertiser or price looks wrong.
- Anything that is not suitable for a church audience.

Third-party adverts show the advertiser, the price and whether it has been paid. The platform fee on the payment is already taken through the normal giving flow.
