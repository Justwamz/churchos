# Dropping off and collecting your children

## Your code
Open **Children** in the app. You see a QR code that changes every 30 seconds. At drop-off, show it to the teacher, who scans it and checks your children in. At pick-up, show it again. A photo of the code will not work.

If your phone is flat or you have no signal, ask the teacher to look you up by name. You will be sent an SMS with a pick-up code.

## While your child is at class
You get a message when the child is checked in, with the tag number, and another when they are collected and by whom. The Children page shows which class they are in.

## Who can collect
You and the other guardians of your child can collect. To let someone else collect, add them under **People who can collect your children** with their name and phone number. When they come, the teacher sends a code to their phone and they read it out. Remove them any time.

## If you are late
After the class ends you will be told by SMS, WhatsApp, email and in the app. Please come, or call the church office.
