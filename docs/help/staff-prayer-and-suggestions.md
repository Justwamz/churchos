# The prayer team

You need the right **Look after prayer requests**. The owner and the pastor have it. The branch admin does not, so private requests are not read by people who are not on the team.

**Prayer** shows the requests sent to the prayer team only, and everything shared with groups, the branch and the church. You can mark a request **answered**, or **hide** one that should not be shown, with a reason. The author is told and still sees it. A request sent without a name shows as **A member** to everyone: you cannot tell who wrote it. Please keep what you read private.

# The suggestion box

You need the right **Read and answer suggestions**. **Suggestions** lists what members sent. A suggestion sent without a name shows as **Anonymous** and cannot be traced, but your reply still reaches the person who sent it. Set a status (under review, done, not possible) and write a reply. Done and not possible need a reply. Switch on **Share this answer with the church** to show it to the branch under **You said, we did**. You can download the list as a CSV.

# Pastors and counsellors: meeting links

Members book a pastor or counsellor from the normal bookings. Once you have approved the booking, open it and add a **meeting link**: paste a Google Meet, Zoom or Jitsi link. Only the member and the people who look after the booking can see it, and the member is told. The person the booking is with, and a manager, can add or change it. There is no video inside ChurchOS and nothing is recorded.

# Helplines

Members see a notice about what is private, and a list of emergency numbers and helplines, when they book a pastor and on the prayer screen. People who manage every branch can edit the list in the booking settings. The standard numbers are only a start: please check each one is right for your area.
