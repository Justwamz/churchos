# The SMS wallet

Payment receipts and pledge reminders can be sent by SMS. Your church pays for them from a prepaid SMS wallet.

## Topping up
Enter an amount (KES 100 to KES 50,000) and the phone that will pay, and approve the prompt on the phone. The money goes to your SMS wallet only. It is kept apart from your collections and never affects your payouts. Each message costs the rate shown on the page.

## What happens when it runs out
Nothing breaks. Gifts are still received, and members still get an in-app notification. SMS is simply switched off until you top up, and you see a warning. You also see a low-balance notice before it runs out.

## The message log
The page lists recent messages and whether each was **sent** or **skipped because the wallet was empty**.
