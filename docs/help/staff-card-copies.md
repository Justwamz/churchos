# Card copies and fees

## Printing a card
Open an approved baptism record and choose **Issue and print**. The first card for a record is **free**. Every issue is logged with who asked for it.

## Copies
Later copies follow your church's price, set under **Certificate design**.

- If the price is zero, the copy is issued straight away.
- If the price is above zero, the copy waits for payment. The person pays through your church's giving page using the link ChurchOS shows you, and the copy becomes available as soon as the payment arrives. A small ChurchOS fee applies to the payment like any other gift.

## Waiving the fee
Someone who can approve records can **waive** the fee for a copy, for example for a member in hardship. A waiver is logged with the name of the person who waived it.

## What the preview watermark means
A card shown before it is issued carries a **PREVIEW** watermark. The watermark is not on an issued card.

## Marriage certificates
ChurchOS does not print marriage certificates. They are legal documents issued by the registry. Open the uploaded scan to see your copy.
