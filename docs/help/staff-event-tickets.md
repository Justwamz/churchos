# Selling tickets for an event

An event can be **free** (people reply Going or Not going) or have **tickets**. Tickets suit a dinner, a concert or a conference where you want a count, a limit, and a way to check people in at the door.

## Set up tickets
1. Create the event as usual, then in the form choose **Tickets**.
2. Add one or more **ticket types**, for example Regular, Couple, Student. For each one set a name, a price (0 for free), how many are available, and the most one person can buy at a time. You can also set when sales open and close.
3. Upload the **poster**. It must be widescreen 16:9, at least 1280 x 720, and no larger than 1 MB. It appears on the ticket page, in the email and in the app.
4. Save.

A free ticket still gets a QR code, so you can use the same check-in at the door and count who comes.

## Who can buy
Anyone. Members buy in the app. Everyone else uses your **share link** (open **Tickets and sales** on the event and choose **Copy**). A non-member gives their name, phone number and email. There is no cash sale: tickets are paid by M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash or card.

## Who pays the fee
The buyer. A small service fee is added on top of the ticket price and shown before they pay. You receive the full ticket price.

## Never oversold
While someone is paying, their tickets are held for 15 minutes. If they do not pay, the tickets are released. The number sold plus held can never be more than the number you set. If you change the number, it cannot go below what is already sold or held. A ticket type that has sales cannot be removed, only switched off.

## What the buyer gets
An email with every ticket and its QR code, and for members the tickets in the app under **My tickets**. Each ticket has its own QR code and works once. The email and the app both link to a ticket page that can be printed.

## Sales and attendees
**Tickets and sales** shows, for each ticket type, how many are sold and left, the takings and how many have come in. Below it is the list of attendees. You can search, filter, download it as a CSV, send a buyer their tickets again, or cancel a ticket.

## Cancelling a ticket or the event
Cancel a ticket with a reason. The buyer is told and the ticket stops working. If they paid, arrange the refund through **Payment help**. Money is not returned automatically. If you cancel the whole event, every buyer is told and you see how many paid orders need a refund.

## Adverts
An advert can lead straight to buying tickets: choose **Sell tickets for an event** when you create it.
