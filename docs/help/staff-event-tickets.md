# Selling tickets for an event

An event can be **free** (people reply Going or Not going) or have **tickets**. Tickets suit a dinner, a concert or a conference where you want a count, a limit, and a way to check people in at the door.

## Set up tickets
1. Create the event as usual, then in the form choose **Tickets**.
2. Add one or more **ticket types**, for example Regular, Couple, Student. For each one set a name, a price (0 for free), how many are available, and the most one person can buy at a time. You can also set when sales open and close.
3. Upload the **poster**. It must be widescreen 16:9, at least 1280 x 720, and no larger than 1 MB. It appears on the ticket page, in the email and in the app.
4. Save.

A free ticket still gets a QR code, so you can use the same check-in at the door and count who comes.

## Who can buy
Anyone. Members buy in the app. Everyone else uses your **share link** (open **Tickets and sales** on the event and choose **Copy**). A non-member gives their name, phone number and email. There is no cash sale: tickets are paid by M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash or card.

## Who pays the fee
The buyer. A small service fee is added on top of the ticket price and shown before they pay. You receive the full ticket price.

## Never oversold
While someone is paying, their tickets are held for 15 minutes. If they do not pay, the tickets are released. The number sold plus held can never be more than the number you set. You can raise the number at any time, even after sales start, for example when the venue can take more people. Say why in the reason box: it is recorded. The number cannot go below what is already sold or held. A ticket type that has sales cannot be removed, only switched off.

## What the buyer gets
An email with every ticket and its QR code, and for members the tickets in the app under **My tickets**. Each ticket has its own QR code and works once. The email and the app both link to a ticket page that can be printed.

## Sales and attendees
**Tickets and sales** shows, for each ticket type, how many are sold and left, the takings and how many have come in. Below it is the list of attendees. You can search, filter, download it as a CSV, send a buyer their tickets again, or cancel a ticket.

## Refunds
In the ticket setup choose **Refunds**: either no refunds, or buyers can ask for a refund until a number of days before the event (30, for example). A buyer cancels a whole order from their ticket page or the app and asks for the money back.

Each request waits for you in **Tickets and sales** under **Refund requests**. Choose **Approve** or **Decline**. If you decline, write a reason: the buyer sees it. While a request is waiting, the buyer's tickets still exist but the door holds them back, so ask a manager before letting them in. Approving cancels the tickets and asks ChurchOS to send the money back to the number or card used. ChurchOS pays it, and you see "Waiting for ChurchOS to send the money" until it has. The buyer is told at each step. Free tickets are simply given back by the buyer, with nothing to approve.

An order with a ticket already used at the door cannot be refunded in full.

## Postponing an event
Choose **Postpone** on the event. Say the new date, or that the date is not decided yet. Add a note to explain why.
- Nothing is refunded automatically. Tickets stay valid for the new date.
- Every buyer is told by email, SMS and in the app. People who replied that they are going are told in the app.
- While there is no new date, tickets are not sold and the door shows "Event postponed".
- Buyers who cannot make it can ask for a refund. While the date is not set they can ask at any time. After you set it, they can ask for a number of days (14 by default).
When you know the date, choose **Set new date**. Sales start again and everyone is told.

## Cancelling a ticket or the event
Cancel a ticket with a reason. The buyer is told and the ticket stops working. If they paid, arrange the refund through **Help with a payment**. Money is not returned automatically. If you cancel the whole event, every buyer is told and you see the paid orders that need a refund. Choose **Refund** next to an order, or **Refund everyone**, to ask ChurchOS to send the money back.

## Making an event free after sales began
Once tickets have been sold, an event cannot become free. Cancel it, refund the buyers, then create a new free event. **Copy as a new event** starts the new one with the same title, place and times.

## Adverts
An advert can lead straight to buying tickets: choose **Sell tickets for an event** when you create it.
