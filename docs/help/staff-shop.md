# Running the church shop

The **Shop** page lets a branch sell things such as Bibles, rosaries and books, and keep track of what is on the shelf. Each branch has its own stock. Pick the branch at the top right if you look after more than one.

## Selling at the till
1. Open **Till** and tap the items. Use the search or the category buttons to find them.
2. Change quantities in the basket. You cannot add more than is in stock.
3. Optionally name the buyer: search for a member, or type a name.
4. Choose how they pay:
   - **Cash.** The sale is complete at once. Type the amount given to see the change.
   - **M-Pesa, Airtel Money or T-Kash.** Enter the buyer's phone number for the right network. They get a prompt on their phone. The items are held for 30 minutes, and the sale completes when the payment arrives.
   - **Card.** Open the card page for the buyer to pay. Same 30 minute hold.
5. Print the receipt, or start a new sale.

A sale that is not paid within 30 minutes is released and the items go back on the shelf. You can also cancel a waiting sale yourself.

## Items and stock
Under **Items and stock** you add items with a selling price, an optional cost price and a low stock level.

- **Receive stock** when new goods arrive. Add what each one cost if you know it.
- **Adjust** to correct a count, with a reason.
- **Write off** damaged or lost items, with a reason.
- **History** shows every movement for an item: who, when, how many and why. Movements are never edited or deleted.

Stock cannot go below zero. Items below their low stock level are marked **Low**, and an empty one **Out**.

## Mistakes
A manager can **void** a cash sale within 24 hours, with a reason, and put the items back. A sale paid by mobile money or card cannot be voided here. Ask for the payment to be reversed in **Payment help**.

## Closing the till
At the end of the day open **Close till**. It shows what the day's cash sales say there should be. Count the cash and enter it. If it differs, write a note. The count then goes to **Cash** for a second person to sign off, and the platform fee on cash is charged there, like any other cash.

## Reports
**Reports** shows takings by day, best sellers, sales by category and payment method, what is running low, and the value of stock at cost. Cost and margin are shown only to managers.

## Who can do what
- **See the shop**: items, stock and sales.
- **Sell at the till**: sell and close the till.
- **Manage the shop**: items, prices, stock, voids, cost and margin.
