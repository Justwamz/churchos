# Answering bookings and the calendar

## Requests that need an answer
Open **Bookings**. **Needs an answer** lists requests you can decide: those for resources in the branches you manage, and, for a person, requests for your own diary. Nobody can answer their own request.

- **Approve** a free booking and it is confirmed. A priced booking becomes **Waiting for payment** and the time is held for 24 hours.
- **Decline** needs a short reason, so the member can choose another time.
- A time that is already booked cannot be approved. Pending requests do not block each other, so approving one may make another impossible.

## Booking several things together
A member can ask for more than one resource at once, for example a pastor and the sanctuary for a marriage day. All of them must be free at the same time, and each is answered by its own person. The booking is approved only when every one has said yes. If one says no, the whole booking is declined with that reason.

## Payment and holds
Once paid, the booking is confirmed. If it is not paid within 24 hours the time is released and the member is told. The platform fee applies to the payment.

## Cancelling and finishing
You can cancel a booking at any time with a reason. The member is told. If they paid, arrange any refund through **Payment help**, since money is not returned automatically. After the time has passed, mark a confirmed booking **Done** or **No show**.

## Notes are private
Members can add a note. Only the member and the people who answer that request can read it. It is never written to the audit log.

## The calendar
**Calendar** shows confirmed and waiting-for-payment bookings together with events, for the branches you manage. Use the resource filter to see one hall or one pastor.
