# Messages about your children

Children never get SMS, WhatsApp or email from the church. They do not have a sign-in either.

When the church sends a message meant for children, it comes to you as their guardian. It starts with **For Ruth:** or **For Ruth and Sam:** so you know who it is about. You get one copy however many children you have in the group, on the channels you have chosen, and in your inbox.

Every child must have at least one guardian. If you are the only guardian of a child you cannot move to another church until another guardian is added.
