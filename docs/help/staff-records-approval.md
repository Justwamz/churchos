# Baptism and marriage records and their approval

## Creating a record
Open **Records** and choose **New record**.

- **Baptism:** enter the person baptised (pick an existing member, or type a name for someone outside your church), the date, place and officiant. Parents, sponsors and a register number are optional.
- **Marriage:** enter both parties, the date, place, officiant and the number printed on the marriage certificate. Upload the **scan or photo** of the certificate (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 3 MB). A marriage certificate is a legal document issued elsewhere, so ChurchOS keeps your copy and does not print one.

Choose **Save as draft** to finish later, or **Send for approval**.

## Approval by a second person
Every record must be approved by a **different person** from the one who entered or last changed it. This protects the church and the people named. Approvers see the record under **Needs your approval**, and can approve it or reject it with a reason. A rejected record returns to the person who entered it so they can fix it and send it again.

When a record is approved it receives a unique **serial number**.

## Changing an approved record
Edit it as usual. The approved version stays in force, and your changes are marked **Changes waiting for approval** until a different person approves them. If they are rejected, the approved record is unchanged. The serial number never changes.

## What members and other churches see
Members see their own approved records in the member app. If a member moves to another church, the record stays with the church that holds it, and the new church sees a read-only copy.
