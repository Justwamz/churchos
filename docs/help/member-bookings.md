# Booking a pastor, a hall or equipment

## Make a booking
1. On **Home**, tap **Book**.
2. Choose what you want: a person, a place or a thing.
3. Pick a day and how long you need, then pick one of the free times.
4. Say what it is for, add a note if you like, and send the request.

Your note is private. Only you and the people who answer your request can read it.

## What happens next
- Some things are **confirmed straight away**.
- Others need the church to say yes. You will get a message, and you can see the answer under **My bookings**.
- If it costs money, you will see **Pay** once it is approved. Pay within 24 hours to keep the time, or it is released for someone else.

## Booking more than one thing
You can add another resource at the same time, for example the hall and a pastor. Every one must be free then, and each is answered separately. If one says no, the whole booking is declined.

## Cancelling
You can cancel until 24 hours before the start. After that, please contact the church. If you already paid, the church will arrange any refund with you.

## If you are the pastor or official
If a diary is yours, you also see **My diary** with the requests for you to approve or decline.
