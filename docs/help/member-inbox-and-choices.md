# Your inbox, message choices and the app

## Your inbox
Messages from your church, receipts for your giving and reminders all arrive in your **Inbox**. A badge shows how many you have not read.

## Choosing how your church reaches you
Open your message choices:

- **WhatsApp messages from my church** is **off** until you turn it on. Only turn it on if you want your church to message you on WhatsApp.
- **SMS messages from my church** is on. Turn it off if you prefer not to get church SMS.

Receipts for your own giving are always sent. If you are a guardian, messages about your children reach you on the choices you have set.

## Installing the app
ChurchOS can be installed on your phone like any other app. On Android and desktop, tap **Install app** when you see it. On iPhone, open the page in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The installed app opens quickly and works for reading what you have already loaded when you are offline.

## If you lead a group
Open **My groups**. As a leader you can message everyone in the group or chosen people, by in-app message, SMS or WhatsApp. You see names and what a message will cost, never anyone's phone number.
