# Pledges to a church project

A pledge is a promise to give a certain amount to a project over time. It is not a payment. You pay in instalments, whenever you can.

## Make a pledge
Open **Give**, choose a project, and use **Make a pledge**. Enter the amount you intend to give.

## Pay towards a pledge
Give to the same project using the same phone number. Your payments count towards your pledge automatically, and the progress bar on **Home** moves.

## Reminders
Your church may send reminders. You get them in the app, and by SMS when the church has SMS credit.
