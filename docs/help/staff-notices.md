# Publishing notices

Notices appear on the **Notice board** in the member app. Use them for news that members should be able to find again later: service times, parking, harvest Sunday, a change of venue.

## Publish a notice
1. Open **Notices** and choose **New notice**.
2. Write a short headline (up to 80 characters) and the text (up to 2,000).
3. Choose who it is for: the **whole church**, **one branch** or **one group**. You can only choose what you manage, and only people who manage every branch can write to the whole church.
4. Optionally **pin** it so it stays at the top, set when it should start showing, and when it should stop.
5. Tick **Send members an alert in the app** if you want a message in their inbox when it goes live. This never sends an SMS and costs nothing. For SMS or WhatsApp use **Messages**.

## Scheduled and ended notices
A notice with a start time in the future is **Scheduled** and members cannot see it yet. When its end time passes it is **Ended** and disappears from the member app.

## Changing and taking down
**Edit** changes the text straight away. **Archive** removes it from the member app at once and cannot be undone. Every publish, change and archive is in the audit log.

## Who sees it
Adults with the member app who belong to the audience you chose. Parents and guardians see notices on behalf of their children, because children have no app.
