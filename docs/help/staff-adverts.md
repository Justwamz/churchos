# Adverts on the member app

Adverts appear in a slider at the top of the member app's home screen. Use them for events such as a carol service or youth movie night, or let a person or business pay to advertise, for example a couple announcing a wedding.

## Create an advert
1. Open **Adverts** and choose **New advert**.
2. Add a short headline (up to 60 characters) and optional text (up to 160). You can add a link members can open.
3. Upload the artwork. It must be **widescreen 16:9**, at least 800 pixels wide (1280 x 720 is ideal), a PNG, JPG or WebP file, and no larger than 1 MB. The preview shows how it will look on a phone.
4. Choose who sees it: the **whole church**, **one branch** or **one group**. You can only choose what you manage.
5. Choose **Run until I stop it**, or pick a start and end date.

## Approval
Someone else must approve every advert. You cannot approve one you created or sent. Choose **Send for approval**. The approver sees it under **Needs my approval** and either approves it or says why not. If it needs changes, edit it and send it again. Changing an advert that is already live takes it offline until it is approved again.

## Adverts that someone pays for
Switch on **This is a paid advert from someone else** and enter the advertiser and the price. After approval you get a **payment link** to send them. They pay by M-Pesa or card and the advert goes live as soon as the payment arrives. The normal platform fee applies to the payment, and the money is part of your collections like any other.

## While it runs
You can **Pause**, **Resume** or **End** an advert. Each card shows how many people saw it, how many tapped it and how many different members viewed it. Each member counts once a day.

## If ChurchOS takes it down
ChurchOS can remove an advert that breaks the rules. You will be told why in the app. A taken-down advert cannot be switched back on. Fix the problem and create a new one.
