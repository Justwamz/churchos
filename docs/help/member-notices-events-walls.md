# Notice board, events and group walls

## Notice board
On **Home** you see the latest notices from your church, branch and groups. Pinned notices stay at the top. Choose **See all** for the full list, and tap a notice to read it.

## Events
**Coming up** on Home shows the next events for you. Tap one to read the details. If the church asked for replies, choose **Going** or **Not going**. You can bring up to 10 guests, and the places left are shown when the event has a limit. If an event is full you can still change to Not going. You get a reminder the day before, and a message if it moves or is cancelled.

## Group walls
Open **Groups** and choose a group to see its wall. Only people in the group can see it, including the leaders and you. Church staff can see that the group exists and how big it is, but not what is posted.

- **Post** something for the group, or **comment** on a post.
- **Delete** your own posts and comments at any time.
- Leaders can **pin** posts, post **announcements**, and remove posts or comments that do not belong. In some groups only leaders can post, and everyone can still comment.

## Joining a group
Some groups are open to requests. Choose **Find a group**, then **Ask to join**. A leader approves or declines, and you get a message either way. You can withdraw a waiting request. Other groups are by invitation only.
