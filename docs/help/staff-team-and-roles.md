# Your team, roles and permissions

The **Team** page is where you decide who can do what in the staff app.

## How access works
Each person has one or more **roles**, and each role applies to a **place**: the whole church, a region, or one branch. A treasurer for Grace Nairobi sees money for Nairobi only. The same person can be a pastor for one branch and a secretary for another.

## Add someone
1. Open **Team** and choose **Add team member**.
2. Enter their full name and phone number.
3. Choose a role and where it applies.

They sign in with that phone number and a code we send. If the number already belongs to a member, their name stays as it is. If the role can see messaging wallets, they will be asked to confirm an email address.

## Give someone more access, or take it away
Choose **Add access** next to a person to give another role for another place. To take a role away, use the **x** on its label. It stops working straight away.

## You can only give what you have
You can give access only for places you look after, and only roles whose every permission you hold there yourself. A branch admin can add a treasurer for their own branch, but not an auditor or a whole-church role. If a request is refused, nothing is created.

## Your church always has an owner
The last **Organisation Owner** cannot be removed. Give the role to someone else first.

## The built-in roles
- **Organisation Owner**: everything.
- **Branch Admin**: everything except payout accounts and a few settings.
- **Treasurer**: collections, settlements, cash, projects and wallets.
- **Pastor**: members, records, messages and groups.
- **Secretary**: members, records and groups.
- **Auditor**: read-only access to money, members, records and the audit log.

Built-in roles cannot be changed.

## Make your own role
Open **Roles** and choose **New role**. Give it a name and tick what it can do. Permissions are listed in plain words and grouped, for example Money, Members, Messaging and Bookings. Only someone who manages every branch can create or change roles, and you can only include permissions you have yourself. A role that is in use cannot be deleted.

Everything is recorded in the audit log: who gave or took away which access, and for where.
