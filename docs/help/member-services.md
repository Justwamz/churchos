# Services

Open **Services** to see the coming services of your church.

## Check in
Check-in opens 20 minutes before the service and closes 2 hours after the start. Choose **I'm here**. Allow location when the app asks: it is used only to confirm that you are at the church. If your phone cannot tell where it is, show an usher your code instead, or choose that you are watching online.

## Your usher code
At the top you have a code that changes every 30 seconds. Show it to an usher and they scan you in.

## The reading of the day
The church can share the reading, theme and notices for each service. You see them in the app when the church releases them (it may be a day before, or just before the service) and right after you check in.

## Bring a friend
Choose **Bring a friend** on a service. Send the link by WhatsApp or show the code to the person beside you. When they check in, the church knows you invited them. You can see how many friends you have brought.

## Watch live
If the service is on YouTube, choose **Watch live**. You are counted as watching online. **Share the live link** sends a link your friends can use to watch.
