# Counting cash and the second sign-off

Cash stays with your church. ChurchOS records it so that all giving is in one place.

## How it works
1. One person enters the **counted amount** on the Cash page and chooses the purpose.
2. The entry shows **Counted by [name], waiting for a second person**.
3. A **different** person with permission to approve cash checks the count and taps **Approve**, or **Reject** if it is wrong.

The same person cannot count and approve. This protects both of them.

## The fee on cash
When the count is approved, ChurchOS calculates the platform fee under your church's fee setting and deducts it from your church's balance. If your balance is too low at that moment, the fee is taken from your next payout.

## Correcting a mistake
An approved count cannot be edited. Reject a wrong count before it is approved, and record a new one.
