# Messaging settings

This is where your church chooses how messaging works. ChurchOS provides the SMS and WhatsApp service, so there are no accounts or passwords to set up. You only choose who pays and how you want to appear.

## Who pays for messages
Choose **shared** (one SMS and one WhatsApp wallet for everyone) or **per branch** (each branch is its own cost centre). Switching to per branch creates an empty SMS and WhatsApp wallet for each branch. Headquarters can then move credit to them. Switching back removes nothing. This applies straight away.

## SMS sender name
The name members see when you send an SMS, 3 to 11 letters and numbers, starting with a letter.

## WhatsApp
Your display name and business number, with the country code, for example `254700123456`. WhatsApp messages go only to people who agreed to them.

## ChurchOS approves sender names and numbers
When you change a sender name, a WhatsApp number or a display name, it becomes a **request**. It shows as **Pending** and the old value keeps being used. ChurchOS usually decides within one working day and tells you in the app. If it is not approved you will see the reason, and you can send a new request. This keeps messages from carrying names that could mislead members.

## Branch overrides
A branch can have its own sender name and WhatsApp number. They are approved in the same way. Leave them empty to use the church's.

## Alerts
Everyone who can see a wallet is alerted when it runs low or empty, in the app and by email. Each of them must have a confirmed email address, see **Confirming your email**. You can also add up to five extra email addresses here, for example a finance mailbox.

## Prices
Prices are set by ChurchOS and shown on the **Wallets** page.

## Send a test
**Send me a test SMS** or **Send me a test WhatsApp message** sends one message to your own number so you can see it works. It costs one message at your current price. A WhatsApp test needs your own agreement first: turn it on in **My profile**.

## Who can change it
Only people with permission to manage messaging settings, normally the organisation owner. Others can see the settings.
