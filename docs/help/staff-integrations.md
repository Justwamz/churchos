# The Integrations page

This is where your church sets up how messaging works.

## Who pays for messages
Choose **shared** (one SMS and one WhatsApp wallet for everyone) or **per branch** (each branch is its own cost centre). Switching to per branch creates an empty SMS and WhatsApp wallet for each branch. Headquarters can then move credit to them. Switching back removes nothing.

## SMS sender name
The name members see when you send an SMS, 3 to 11 letters and numbers, starting with a letter.

## WhatsApp
Your display name and business number, with the country code, for example `254700123456`. WhatsApp messages go only to people who agreed to them.

## Branch overrides
A branch can have its own sender name and WhatsApp number. Leave them empty to use the church's.

## Alerts
Everyone who can see a wallet is alerted automatically when it runs low or empty, in the app and by email if they have added an email to **My profile**. You can also add up to five extra email addresses here, for example a finance mailbox.

## Send a test
**Send me a test SMS** or **Send me a test WhatsApp message** sends one message to your own number so you can see it works. It costs one message. A WhatsApp test needs your own agreement first: turn it on in **My profile**.

## Who can change it
Only people with permission to manage integrations, normally the organisation owner. Others can see the settings.
