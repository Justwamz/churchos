# Recording attendance at the door

You need the right to **Record attendance at the door**. Open **Usher** when the service is on. It lists the services whose check-in is open.

## Scan a member
Choose **Scan a member** and point the camera at the code on the member's phone (**Services** in the member app, *My usher code*). It changes every 30 seconds, so a photo does not work. A green message says welcome. Amber means they were already counted.

## Find a member
No phone? Choose **Find a member**, type the name or member number, and choose **Mark present**.

## Add a visitor
Choose **Add a visitor**. Type their name and phone number. Both are needed. A first-time visitor is sent a welcome SMS automatically. If the number belongs to a member, they are counted as that member.

## The headcount
Type how many adults, children and visitors are in the room, then save. This is your reliable total. The report compares it with the check-ins to show how many people are covered. You can also type the highest number of people watching online, from YouTube.

## The door screen
Choose **Door screen** and put the tablet or screen by the door. It shows a QR code that changes every 45 seconds. Visitors scan it with their ordinary phone camera, type their name and number, and are counted. A photo of the code stops working after a few minutes.
