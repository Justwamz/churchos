# Checking that a certificate is genuine

## Scan the QR code
Every baptism card issued through ChurchOS carries a QR code and a serial number. Scan the code with your phone camera. It opens a page that tells you whether the certificate is real.

## What the page shows
If the card is genuine you see a green confirmation with the name, date and place, the church that issued it, and the serial number. Check that the name matches the person presenting the card.

## If it says it cannot be verified
The card may be damaged, altered, or not issued through ChurchOS. Contact the church named on the card. For privacy, the page gives the same answer whether or not a serial number exists.
