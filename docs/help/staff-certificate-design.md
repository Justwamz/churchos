# Designing your baptism card

Open **Certificate design** to set up the card once. Every baptism card your church prints uses it.

## What you can set
- **Church name, tagline and title.**
- **Logo.** PNG, JPG or SVG, up to 500 KB.
- **Wording.** Write the sentence the card shows. Use placeholders and ChurchOS fills them in: `{name}`, `{date}`, `{place}`, `{officiant}`, `{church}`, `{parents}`, `{sponsors}`.
- **Signatories.** Up to three names with their titles, printed under signature lines.
- **Accent colour, footer note,** and whether to show the **QR code**.
- **Price of a copy.** Free, or between KES 10 and KES 5,000. See *Card copies and fees*.

## Address and contact details
These are kept for each branch, so a card shows the details of the branch that issued it. Fill them in under the design form.

## The live preview
The card on the right updates as you type, so you see exactly what will print. The preview shows sample details, not a real person.

## Who can change it
Only roles with permission to manage templates can save changes. Everyone else can see the design.
