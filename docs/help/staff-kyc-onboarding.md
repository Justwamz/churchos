# Getting your church verified

ChurchOS must verify your church before it can pay money out. Until you are verified you can still collect, and the money waits safely until you are approved.

## What to upload
For your whole organisation:
- **Registration certificate**
- **KRA PIN certificate**
- **ID for at least one authorised signatory**

For each branch that will receive payouts:
- **Proof of the payout account**, such as a bank letter or a recent statement.

Files can be PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 3 MB each.

## What happens after you upload
A ChurchOS reviewer approves or rejects each document. If one is rejected, you see the reason. Upload a corrected file and it is reviewed again.

A branch is paid out only when the three organisation documents and its own payout proof are all approved.

## Replacing a document
A newer upload replaces an older one. The newest document is the one that counts, so a replacement must be approved before payouts continue.
