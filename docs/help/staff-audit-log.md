# The audit log and version history

## What it records
Every change in your church is recorded: who did it, what they did, when, which app and device they used, and the reason when they gave one. For changes to details, the log shows the old value and the new value side by side.

It covers members and households, baptism and marriage records, your certificate design, branch addresses, projects, cash counts and sign-offs, reversals, payout account changes, verification documents, and sign-ins. Viewing sensitive information, such as exact dates of birth or an uploaded file, is recorded too.

## Finding something
Open **Audit log**. Search by words, filter by type, person or date, or type the start of an action such as `record.`. Click a row to see everything about the event, including related items such as the payment behind a reversal.

## History of one record
Many screens have a **History** button, for example on a record or a member. It shows every saved version with who changed it and when. Pick any two versions and choose **Compare** to see only the fields that differ.

## Entries cannot be changed
Nobody can edit or delete an entry, not even ChurchOS staff. Each entry is linked to the one before it. **Verify integrity now** checks the whole chain and tells you exactly where, if anywhere, it does not match.

## Who can see it
The organisation owner, branch admins (for their branches) and anyone given the **Auditor** role. An auditor can read and export the log but cannot change anything. Only some roles can download the CSV.

## Sensitive details are hidden
Phone numbers, dates of birth and account numbers appear partly hidden in the log and in history.
