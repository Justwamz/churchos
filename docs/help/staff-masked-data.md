# Why phone numbers are partly hidden

To protect your members, ChurchOS shows phone numbers partly hidden, for example `254711•••001`. This applies to member lists, collection reports, downloads and message logs. It is part of keeping members' personal information safe under the Data Protection Act.

## Showing a full number
If your role allows it, you see a **Show number** button. When you use it:

1. ChurchOS asks you to confirm and, if you like, say why you need it.
2. The number is shown for 30 seconds and then hides again.
3. The action is written to the audit log with your name and your reason.

If you do not see the button, your role does not include it. Ask your church administrator.

## Messages do not need numbers
When you send a message to a group of people, you choose who it is for, such as all members, and ChurchOS delivers it. You never need to see or copy anyone's number.

## Children
A child has no phone of their own. Their contact numbers are their guardians' numbers, hidden in the same way.

## Editing a number
When you edit an adult member, the phone field starts empty. Leave it blank to keep the current number, or type a new one to replace it.
