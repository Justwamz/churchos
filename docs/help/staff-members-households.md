# Members, households and guardians

## Adults and children
Every member is either an **adult** or a **child**.

- An **adult** needs their own phone number. It is how they sign in and give.
- A **child** has no phone of their own. You choose one or more **guardians** from your existing adult members, for example both parents. The guardians' numbers are the child's contact numbers.
- A child needs a date of birth. On their 18th birthday they become an adult automatically, their guardians are told, and you see them under **Needs their own phone number** until you add it.

## Adding a member
Choose **Add member**, pick adult or child, and fill in the details. You must tick the box confirming the member, or the guardian for a child, agreed to the church keeping these details. Sex and, for adults, date of birth are optional.

## Households
A household simply groups people who belong together, such as a family. It has no name and no head. ChurchOS shows it as, for example, "Wanjiku household", taken from its primary contact.

- A person is in **one household at a time**. If you try to add someone who already has one, ChurchOS asks whether to move them.
- When a child starts their own family, move them to a new household. Their guardian links stay.
- A household can have a **primary contact**, an adult who receives its notices. If they leave, another adult takes over.
- The Members page groups households as expandable rows. Search finds people by name, member number or phone, and shows the whole household.

## Member numbers
Your church keeps its own numbering. The organisation owner sets the format, for example `GRC/{YYYY}/{SEQ:4}`, and the next number. When you add a member, leave the number blank to use the format, or type an existing number. A number is unique within your church organisation and never changes once issued. Changing the format affects only new members.

## Birthdays and ages
You see each person's age band and birthday. The exact date of birth is shown only to roles that have permission for it. Members get an in-app birthday greeting on their birthday.

## What you can see
You only see members of the branches your role covers.
