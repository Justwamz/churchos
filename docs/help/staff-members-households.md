# Members, households and member numbers

## Adding a member
Choose **Add member**, enter the name and branch, and a phone number if they have one. People with a phone number can sign in to the member app with it.

## Member numbers
Each member gets a number that is unique across your whole church organisation, not just their branch. The number stays the same if they move to another branch of your organisation.

## Households
Group a family by giving the household a name, then add each person with their relationship (head, spouse, child). Children without a phone are managed by a guardian and can claim their own account later.

## What you can see
You only see members of the branches your role covers.
