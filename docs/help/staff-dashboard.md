# The live collections dashboard

The dashboard shows what your church has received today, as it happens.

## What you see
- **Today and this week:** the total received and the number of gifts.
- **By method and purpose:** how much came through M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash or card, and how much was tithe, offering or project giving.
- **Live feed:** each new gift appears at the top within seconds. A green dot shows the live connection is working.
- **Balances:** money in the 48 hour hold, money ready to pay out, and any amount owed back after a reversal.

## HQ and branches
If you manage several branches, use the selector at the top. **All branches** shows the roll-up. Click a branch in the table to see only that branch. You only ever see the branches your role covers.

## Cash
Counted cash that has been signed off shows in a separate **recorded cash** line, so you see all giving in one place.
