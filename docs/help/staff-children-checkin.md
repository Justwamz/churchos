# Checking children in and out

You need the **Check children in and out** permission and to be listed as a teacher of the class. You only see your own class.

## Start
1. Open **Children** and choose your class.
2. Choose **Open session** and say when it ends, for example in two hours. This is when parents are expected to collect.

## Drop-off
1. Choose **Scan a guardian** and point the camera at the QR code on the guardian's phone (Children in the member app). The code changes every 30 seconds, so a photo of it will not work.
2. Tick the children being dropped off and choose **Check in**. Write the **tag number** on a sticker for the child and one for the guardian.
3. The guardian is told in the app that the child is with you.

### No app?
Choose **Find by name or phone**, pick the guardian and check the children in. The guardian gets an SMS with the tag and a **pick-up code**.

## Pick-up
- Scan the guardian again and choose **Hand over**. Any registered guardian of the child can collect. If it is not the person who dropped off, the screen tells you, and the person who dropped off is told by SMS.
- With a pick-up code: choose **I have a pick-up code**, type the six digits, then **Hand over**.
- Someone else collecting (an aunt, a driver): choose **Someone else is collecting** next to the child. The people the guardian approved are listed. Choose one: they get a code by SMS on their own phone. Type the code they read out. Nobody who is not on the list can collect a child.

## If a child is not collected
After the finish time plus a few minutes, the guardians are told by SMS, WhatsApp, email and in the app. After a little longer the church office is alerted. You can alert the office yourself at any time with **Alert the church office**. When a child is late, **Call guardians** shows their phone numbers. Showing numbers is recorded.

## Finish
Choose **Close session** once every child has gone home. If someone is still here, the screen tells you who.

Every scan, code and alert is recorded with the time, who scanned and on what device.
