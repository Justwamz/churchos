# How payouts and the 48 hour hold work

## The timeline of a gift
1. **In the 48 hour hold.** A gift is visible straight away, but it cannot be paid out yet. This protects against reversals.
2. **Ready to pay out.** After 48 hours it moves here.
3. **Paid out.** Each day ChurchOS pays everything that is ready to your church's verified payout account, in one payment per branch.

## Why a payout may be smaller than expected
If a gift was reversed after it had already been paid out, the amount is deducted from your next payout. The **owed** balance shows this.

## Why a payout may not arrive
- The branch is **not verified yet**. Money keeps collecting safely, and is paid out once verification is approved. See *Getting your church verified*.
- Payouts were **paused** because the amount owed after reversals is large. A ChurchOS agent will contact you.
- The payment provider declined the payout. Nothing is lost, and ChurchOS retries on the next run.
