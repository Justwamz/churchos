# Events and replies

Events appear as an agenda in the member app, for the audience you choose.

## Create an event
1. Open **Events** and choose **New event**.
2. Add a title, a start time, and optionally an end time, a place and a description.
3. Choose who it is for: the whole church, one branch or one group.
4. Switch on **Ask people to reply** if you need numbers. You can set a **capacity**. Guests count towards it, and each person can bring up to 10.

## Replies and capacity
Members answer **Going** or **Not going**. When the event is full nobody else can say Going, but anyone can still change to Not going, which frees their places. You cannot lower the capacity below the number of people already coming.

Open **Who is coming** to see names and guest counts. Phone numbers are never shown.

## Reminders
Everyone who said they are going gets a reminder in the app 24 hours before the start. If you move the event, they are told, and the reminder is sent again for the new time.

## Moving and cancelling
**Edit** changes the details. **Cancel event** asks for a short reason, closes replies, and tells everyone who said they are going. A cancelled event stays on the agenda marked **Cancelled** until its date has passed.

Past events are under the **Past** tab. Every change is in the audit log.
