# Approving member transfers

When a member asks to join your branch, the request appears in **Transfers**. Only the **receiving** branch decides.

## Approve or reject
- **Approve** adds the member to your branch.
- **Reject** leaves them where they are.

## What changes
- From another branch of your organisation: the member keeps their member number.
- From a different church organisation: your organisation issues a new member number.
- The old church keeps its own history. Nothing is deleted.
