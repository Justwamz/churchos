# Sending messages to your members

You send a message to a **list of people**, never to phone numbers. You choose who it is for and which channels to use, and ChurchOS does the rest. You never see anyone's number.

## Who can send
Sending is a permission that can be given to any role, for the whole church, a region or a branch. You can only message people in the places your role covers.

## Choosing the audience
- **Whole church** (or all of your branches), **a region**, **a branch**, or **a group**.
- Narrow it down: everyone, adults only, or parents and guardians of children. You can also choose age bands and sex.
- Children never receive SMS, WhatsApp or email. They are reached through their guardians, who get one copy however many children they have, starting "For Ruth:". A person who is both a direct recipient and a guardian gets one message, not two.

## Choosing channels
- **In-app** is always included, free.
- **SMS** goes to people with a phone who have not opted out.
- **WhatsApp** goes only to people who have agreed to receive it.

Before you send, ChurchOS shows how many people will be reached on each channel, why some will not (opted out, no phone, no app account, not agreed to WhatsApp) and about what it will cost.

## Writing the message
Use **Insert first name** to personalise it. An SMS over 160 characters is sent as more than one part and costs one price for each part.

## Now or later
Choose **Send now**, or **Schedule for later** and pick a date and time (East Africa Time). A scheduled message can be cancelled until the moment it goes. After it has gone it cannot be recalled.

## After sending
Open any message to see how many were delivered on each channel, and which wallets were short. The cost comes from your church's wallets, branch by branch if your church uses per-branch wallets.
