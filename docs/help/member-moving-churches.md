# Moving to a new church or branch

If you have moved city, you do not need to start again.

## How to move
1. Open the **Church** tab in the member app.
2. Under **Moving to a new church?**, type the code of your new church, for example `GRC002`.
3. Tap **Request transfer**.

The new church must approve your request. You can see its status on the same screen.

## What happens to my record?
- **Same church organisation, different branch:** you keep your member number. Only your branch changes.
- **A different church organisation:** the new church gives you a new member number. You keep your ChurchOS account and phone number.
- Your old church keeps its own records of your giving and attendance. It is never deleted.
- Sacramental records, such as baptism and marriage, belong to the church that holds them.
