# Your baptism card and church records

## Where to find them
Open the **Church** tab and look under **My records**. Records appear once your church has approved them.

## Getting your baptism card
Open the record and choose **Get my card**. Your first card is free. You can print it or save it as a PDF.

A later copy may cost a small fee, set by your church. If so, the app shows the price and a link to your church's giving page. Once the payment arrives, tap **I have paid, check again** and your card appears.

## If you moved to another church
Your record stays with the church that issued it. It still shows in your app, marked with the church that holds it.

## Marriage certificates
A marriage certificate is a legal document issued by the registry. Your church keeps a copy on file, but for a certified copy please ask your church office or the registry.

## How others can check your card
Every card has a QR code. Scanning it opens a page that confirms the card is genuine. The page shows your name, the date and the church, and nothing else.
