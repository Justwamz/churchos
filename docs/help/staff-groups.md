# Groups and group leaders

Groups bring together people who belong to the same ministry, such as a choir, a youth fellowship or a small Christian community.

## Creating a group
Choose **New group**, give it a name and, if it belongs to one branch, choose the branch. A group for the whole church can be created by someone who manages the whole church.

## Adding people
Open a group and choose **Add people**. Search for members, tick several, and choose whether they join as members or as leaders. A leader must be an adult. A group can include people from different branches.

For each person you see how they can be reached, shown as In-app, SMS and WhatsApp. You never see their phone number.

## Group leaders
A leader sees their groups in the member app and can message the whole group, or chosen people in it, by in-app, SMS or WhatsApp. Leaders see names and an estimate of the cost, never numbers or wallet balances. The group's own wallet pays. A leader can reach up to 1,000 people a day.

## Children in a group
Children are reached through their guardians.

## How people join, and the wall
When you create or edit a group choose **How people join**: by invitation only, or members can ask to join and a leader approves. Open groups appear under **Find a group** in the member app.

Every group has a private **wall** in the member app for posts, comments and leader announcements. You can set it so only leaders post. Church staff see the group and its size, never the wall, unless they are in the group themselves.

## Everything is recorded
Creating a group and changing its people is written to the audit log, and so is every message a leader sends.
