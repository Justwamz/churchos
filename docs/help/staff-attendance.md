# Attendance

You need the right to **See attendance and visitors**.

## Last week
The top of the page shows last week's members, first-time visitors, returning visitors and people who watched online, with the change from the week before and the usual number (the average of the four weeks before). More first-time visitors is good news: it means the church is reaching people. If you typed a headcount, you also see how much of it was checked in. The check-in numbers are a sample, so treat them as a guide to the mix, not an exact count.

## First-time visitors
Everyone who gave their name and number for the first time is listed to follow up. Call them, then mark them **Contacted** with a note, or **Not interested**. When they come again they become *returning*, and when they join as members they show as members. If your church has the welcome SMS switched on, the list shows whether it was sent. If a member invited them, you see who.

## Regular members who stopped coming
Members who came in at least three of six weeks and not in the last three. It is only as complete as your check-in coverage, which is shown with last week's numbers.

## The Monday email
Every Monday at 7:00 am you receive last week's report by email: each service, the change on the week before and the usual number, the first-time visitors, the members who stopped coming, events, and children in Sunday school. If you can see every branch, you get the whole church and each branch. Switch it off on this page, or send yourself last week's report now.
