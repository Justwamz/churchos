# Checking people in at the door

The **Check-in** page lets you scan tickets on a phone or tablet. You need the **Check people in at the door** permission. It shows nothing else: no buyers, no money.

## Scan
1. Open **Check-in** and choose the event if you are asked.
2. Choose **Start scanning** and allow the camera. Point it at the QR code on the ticket, on a phone or on paper.
3. The screen answers at once:
   - **Valid** in green, with the name and ticket type. If tickets were bought together you also see how many of that group have arrived, for example "2 of 3".
   - **Already used**, with the time and who scanned it. Do not let them in again.
   - **Cancelled**, **wrong event** or **not found** with the reason.
   - **Event postponed**: do not let anyone in yet.
   - **Refund requested**: the buyer asked for a refund for this ticket. Ask a manager before letting them in.

Each ticket works once. A screenshot of a QR code works only for whoever is scanned first.

## If the camera does not work
Type the code printed under the QR code, for example `GALA01-0001`, and choose **Check in**. The camera works when the page is opened with https, or on the same device as the app. If your venue has poor signal, check that you can load the page before the event starts.

## The count
At the top you see how many have checked in of those sold, and a list of the latest arrivals. Every check-in is recorded in the audit log.
