## What we collect

- **Your account**: name, phone number, email address if you add one.
- **Your church membership**: church and branch, member number, date joined, household, and, if the church records them, date of birth and sex. Children's details are given by a guardian.
- **Giving and payments**: what you gave, when, how, for what, the receipt reference and the phone number the payment came from. If you give anonymously, the church does not see who you are, but ChurchOS keeps a private link so you can see your own history.
- **Events, bookings, shop and credit**: tickets, bookings, orders and event credit.
- **Attendance**: services you check in to, and visitor details if you visit.
- **Things you write**: prayer requests, suggestions, group posts and comments.
- **Your choices**: message consents, and the date you agreed to a church keeping your details.
- **Security**: when and from which device you signed in.

Some of this is sensitive: belonging to a church reveals religious belief, and we hold children's details and baptism and marriage records. We handle them with extra care [and rely on your consent and the church's lawful activities, to confirm with counsel].

## Why we use it

To run your membership; to take, receipt and report gifts; to send messages you agreed to; to keep the books honest and meet tax and accounting law; to keep children safe; to keep your account secure; to answer your requests.

## Who sees it

## Your rights

You can:

- **See and download your data**: member app, Me, Download my data.
- **Correct it**: tell your church, or change your email and message choices in the app.
- **Ask us to delete it**: member app, Me, Delete my account. Your church (or ChurchOS) must approve, and you are told the answer in the app. We remove your name, phone, email, member records, messages and what you wrote. **We must keep** records of gifts and payments, the audit trail, issued statements and church registers for the periods above, but they will no longer be linked to your name. Event credit you hold is lost and regular giving is cancelled.
- **Object or withdraw consent** to messages: Me, Message choices.
- **Complain** to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner: [contact details].

We answer within [30 days, to confirm].
